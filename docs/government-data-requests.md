# Government and third-party requests for Tanatasa customer data

Effective: 31 October 2026  
Operator: WELL IN STYLE PTE. LTD. (Singapore UEN 202445049G)  
Policy owner: Data Protection Officer / business owner  
Contact: hello@tanatasa.com

This policy applies to requests from government agencies, law enforcement, courts, litigants and other third parties for personal data held by Tanatasa, including data created through its shopping connector. It supplements the [Tanatasa Privacy Policy](https://tanatasa.sg/privacy). Customer access, correction and deletion requests follow that privacy policy.

## Where to send a request

Send a written request to hello@tanatasa.com with the requesting organisation, verified official contact details, legal authority, relevant jurisdiction, specific customer or order identifiers, requested data categories, date range and response deadline. Attach the relevant legal process and explain any confidentiality or preservation requirement. Receipt or acknowledgement does not mean that disclosure is authorised. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private shopping-session tokens.

## Review before disclosure

The Data Protection Officer or business owner logs the request in a restricted case record and independently verifies the requester's identity and authority through official channels. The reviewer checks jurisdiction, validity, enforceability, scope and the applicable Singapore data-protection and disclosure requirements. Legal advice is obtained where the authority, jurisdiction or permitted response is unclear.

Informal requests do not by themselves authorise disclosure. We seek clarification, narrowing or appropriate legal process for requests that are unclear, excessive, unsupported or directed to the wrong entity. Where a request appears unlawful or disproportionate, we assess the available grounds to object or challenge it, with legal advice where needed. This policy does not promise that every request can be challenged.

## Preservation and response

A preservation request is reviewed separately from a request to disclose. We preserve only the records covered by a valid preservation duty, restrict access and document its scope and expiry. A preservation hold does not itself authorise disclosure.

Where disclosure is lawfully required or otherwise permitted under applicable law, the owner approves the response and limits it to the data necessary for that request. We use a verified secure delivery channel, record the legal basis, approval, recipient and disclosed data, and retain the case record only as required for accountability and applicable obligations. We do not create a general access channel or share administrative credentials or unrelated customers' records.

We notify affected customers before disclosure where legally permitted and appropriate, unless doing so would create a serious safety risk or compromise a lawful investigation. If notification is legally prohibited, we record the restriction and review whether notice can be given when it ends.

## Emergencies and providers

Claims of imminent danger receive prompt escalation and verification. Any emergency disclosure must still have a lawful basis, be necessary and proportionate, and be documented; an emergency label alone is insufficient.

Data held independently by Stripe, Cloudflare, Resend, Meta/Muse or other providers is subject to their applicable policies and legal obligations. We disclose only data we control and lawfully may disclose. Where appropriate, we direct a requester to the relevant provider without supplying customer data.

## Operational responsibility

Staff and contractors must send requests to the policy owner and must not disclose customer data on their own initiative. The owner maintains a restricted request register, checks the response and records closure or continuing preservation duties. This policy is reviewed when providers, legal requirements or the connector's data handling materially change.
